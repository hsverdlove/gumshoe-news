## Cybersecurity | sting=cyber
[andrew] Now the cyber desk, and if you run a corporate network, this one's for you.
[andrew] CISA, the federal cybersecurity agency, is urging everyone to patch Ivanti Connect Secure V.P.N. appliances right away. Attackers have been exploiting a flaw since at least October first that lets them run commands without logging in. Researchers at Volexity say it's being chained with two other bugs to get full control, and one of those, a privilege escalation flaw, still has no patch. Because the attack runs in memory, Ivanti's own integrity checker may not spot it, so CISA recommends network and memory forensics. Federal agencies have until Friday.
[andrew] Cisco patched another actively exploited zero-day in its Secure Email Gateway. A booby-trapped attachment can give an attacker root access on unpatched devices.
[andrew] And Nextcloud admins: if you use the Collabora built-in CODE server app, update it to version twenty-six point four point three oh three or remove it. Just disabling it doesn't help. Attackers have been planting web shells since September.
[andrew] On the bigger picture, the heads of the I.M.F. and the Bank of England warned that cyber fraud could erode trust in the financial system so badly that people opt out, with the I.M.F.'s Kristalina Georgieva saying some could turn to shoeboxes under the bed. On the defense side, Microsoft and Japanese authorities say they took down a scam network.
