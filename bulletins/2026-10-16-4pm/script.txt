## Cybersecurity | sting=cyber
[andrew] Cyber desk. CISA added two actively exploited flaws to its must-patch list today. One is the BeyondTrust remote support bug we covered this morning. The other is in Fortinet's FortiOS, the software on its FortiGate firewalls. Palo Alto Networks' Unit forty-two says a suspected state-backed group, after breaking into a telecom service provider through Fortinet's management console, used that flaw to plant a new implant called PEERSTITCH.
[andrew] What makes it nasty is that it lives only in memory, opens no ports, and makes no outbound connections. It hides inside two legitimate firewall processes and quietly lets tagged V.P.N. sessions skip security checks. The attackers also stole configurations for more than five hundred managed firewalls. Fortinet patched the flaw in April, so if you run FortiGate gear, check that you're updated and look for signs of past intrusion.
