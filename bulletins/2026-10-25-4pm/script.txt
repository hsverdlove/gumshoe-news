## Cybersecurity | sting=cyber
// Story 1: Tailworm
[ava] On the cyber desk, if you use Tailscale to reach your home or work machines, listen up. Researcher Erica Windisch of Cyberdione Labs released a report called Tailworm, detailed today by The Hacker News, describing flaws in Tailscale's built-in SSH feature that, in her lab, let one already-hacked device get root on other machines its access rules can reach, and spread from there. There's no patch or official advisory yet. Tailscale says it's working on fixes but is "not recommending that everyone disable Tailscale SSH," and some researchers question how "wormable" it really is. What to do now: turn off SSH port forwarding where you can, write access rules as one device to one target, and turn on key expiry.
> source: The Hacker News, "Unpatched Tailscale SSH Flaws Could Let a Hacked Device Reach Root on Other Servers" (Oct 25); Cyberdione Labs, Tailworm report (Oct 25)

## Local News & Events | sting=local
// Story 1: Mulholland lawsuit
[ava] Closer to home, former Londonderry Town Manager Shaun Mulholland is suing the town over his firing on September twenty-first. His suit in Rockingham County Superior Court argues the council's three-to-two vote wasn't enough, since he says the charter requires four votes, thirty days' notice and a chance at a public hearing. He's seeking a jury trial and back pay. The town hasn't had its say in court yet, and the claims haven't been tested; the council majority has relied on the view that state law allows removal by a simple majority.
> source: Londonderry Times, "Town Being Sued Over Breach of Contract, Unlawful Vote" (Chris Paul, Oct 25)
