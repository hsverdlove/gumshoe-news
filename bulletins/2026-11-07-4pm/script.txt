[ava] Story three, an update on the shooting in Krakow, Poland. The suspect, a young Polish man, is in custody undergoing surgery. Officials stress the motive is still under investigation.

## Artificial Intelligence | sting=ai
// Story 6: DeepMind RSA-1024
[andrew] Story six, A I. Google DeepMind says A I agents, working under human supervision with researchers at U C San Diego and France's Inria, found two new weaknesses in legacy ten-twenty-four-bit R S A encryption. One shows that when an attacker controls about half or more of a signed data block, the standard padding adds essentially no protection against forged signatures; a related algorithm is, in theory, around sixteen hundred times better than the previous best attack in a harder case. The other cuts the average cost of cracking each additional key when many ten-twenty-four-bit keys are attacked together, by about thirty times. The important caveat: DeepMind says modern twenty-forty-eight-bit and larger keys, the kind protecting banking, messaging, and software updates, are not affected, and the headline speedups are theoretical estimates. Still, it's another sign A I is speeding up the hunt for cracks in old crypto, so anything still on ten-twenty-four-bit keys should move.
> source: Interesting Engineering, "Google DeepMind agents expose weaknesses in older encryption methods" (Nov 6); Neural Brew and Crypto Briefing summaries of DeepMind's cryptanalysis research (Nov 2026)

## Cybersecurity | sting=cyber
// Story 7: WordPress core flaw
[ava] Story seven, the cyber desk. CISA has flagged an actively exploited flaw in WordPress itself, not a plugin. It's a race condition in core updates for versions four point seven through seven point one point three: during a full core upgrade, temporary staging files under the wp-content upgrade folder can be reached from the web, which can let an unauthenticated attacker run code on the server. The fix is WordPress seven point one point four or the patched release for your branch. The Hacker News points out a catch: installing the fix is itself an update, so security teams advise blocking web access to the wp-content slash upgrade folder first, confirming it returns a forbidden error, and then updating. Afterwards, check logs and files for signs of access during recent upgrades. CISA's deadline for federal agencies has already passed.
> source: CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities alert (Nov 3); BleepingComputer, "CISA flags WordPress RCE flaw as actively exploited in attacks"; The Hacker News, "Installing the Fix for an Exploited WordPress Flaw Can Expose Some Sites One Last Time" (Nov 2026)

## Uplifting Story | sting=feel
[ava] And finally, from Somerset, England. Kim Reynolds couldn't figure out why her doberman, Saraya, wouldn't leave six-month-old Alexander's side one morning, crying whenever he was carried away. When the baby started looking unwell, Kim took him to the hospital, where doctors found his heart racing at two hundred eighty beats a minute. He was diagnosed with Wolff-Parkinson-White syndrome, which is manageable when treated but can be deadly if missed, and he's now doing well on daily medication. Saraya isn't a trained assistance dog, but this month she fussed over him again, and it turned out to be tonsillitis. "Now, I one hundred percent trust Saraya," Kim says. Good dog.
