## Artificial Intelligence | sting=ai
// Story 10: OpenAI matrix multiplication claim; Claude desktop apps
[andrew] Story ten, AI. OpenAI has posted nine more math papers written with an unreleased internal model. The headline claim: the exponent for multiplying large matrices is exactly nine-fourths, two point two five. Faster methods approaching that number were already known.
