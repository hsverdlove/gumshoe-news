// Story 2: Chinese connected-car ban near final vote
[ava] Story two. Congress is close to permanently banning cars tied to China from American roads. Senators Bernie Moreno, a Republican from Ohio, and Elissa Slotkin, a Michigan Democrat, have reached a revised deal on the Connected Vehicle Security Act. It would bar sales of connected vehicles, and the software and hardware inside them, linked to China, Russia, Iran or North Korea. Politico says a vote could come in December, before Democrats take control of Congress.
[ava] The latest changes exempt passive Chinese shareholders, so brands like Volvo and Mercedes-Benz can keep selling here. Backers cite the risk of car data flowing to foreign governments. Critics call it protectionism that keeps cheaper EVs out.
> source: Politico, "China car ban at the 'one yard line' in Congress" (Nov 26); Chosun Biz, "US lawmakers seek to pass bipartisan bill restricting Chinese auto parts" (Nov 27); Economy Media (Nov 2026)

## Technology | sting=tech
// Story 6: Tesla EU vote; Poland-Palantir
[andrew] Story six, tech. Tesla's driver assistance could get a Europe-wide decision next month. A draft agenda for the EU's Technical Committee on Motor Vehicles lists a possible vote on December fifteenth on letting the Netherlands grant EU-wide approval for what's called Tesla Assisted Driving, the European version of Full Self-Driving Supervised. A vote isn't guaranteed, and approval needs a qualified majority of member states.

Palantir's Alex Karp attended the signing in Warsaw. Poland's Defense Ministry says data, servers and encryption keys stay under Polish military control. Critics in Poland flag Palantir's surveillance record. Supporters say the front line state needs the best tools available.

## Artificial Intelligence | sting=ai
// Story 7: Canada compute fund; Google Workspace agent rules
[andrew] Story seven, AI. Canada is adding seven hundred million Canadian dollars to its AI Compute Access Fund, bringing it to one billion. Small and mid-sized Canadian companies can get up to eighty percent of their computing costs covered if they buy from sovereign Canadian providers, or up to half from other Canadian-based providers.
[andrew] And if you use AI assistants with Gmail, Google is changing the rules. Starting February first, AI agents that work with Gmail, Drive and Calendar will have to connect through Google's official Workspace connectors or command-line tool, rather than calling the older interfaces directly. Google says it gives organizations more control. Developers will have some rewiring to do.
> source: Government of Canada, "$700 million expansion of the AI Compute Access Fund" (Nov 2026); BetaKit (Nov 2026); Google Workspace Updates blog, "Simplifying agent access while keeping organizations in control" (Nov 2026); The Neuron, AI digest (Nov 27)

## Cybersecurity | sting=cyber
// Story 8: Air Force Cloud One breach; Microsoft Store Vinci RAT
[ava] Story eight, the cyber desk, and it's a big one. Reuters reports that hackers breached Cloud One, the Air Force's flagship cloud computing platform, according to three people familiar with the matter. Cloud One combines services from Microsoft, Amazon, Oracle and Google and runs scores of applications, from personnel and pay to a system that tracks bombs and missiles at more than two hundred Air Force locations.
[ava] The intrusion was found within the past month during upgrade work. One source says the attackers used a software flaw to reach the control plane, which he compared to a universal remote. Reuters couldn't determine who did it, or whether any data was stolen. Migrations to the platform have been paused, updates blocked and some administrator rights revoked, and Cyber Command has been called in. The Air Force says it doesn't comment on specific networks.
[ava] Closer to home, the security firm eSentire found that a free Microsoft Store app called Setup Assistant was actually a backdoor that installed remote control malware called Vinci RAT on Windows PCs. The store page has since disappeared. If you installed it, remove it and run a full scan.
> source: Reuters via HuffPost and CNBC, "Hackers breach U.S. Air Force flagship Cloud One computing platform" (Nov 27, 6:35 a.m. ET); Bloomberg (Nov 27); eSentire, "Vinci RAT Delivered Through a Malicious Microsoft Store App" (Nov 2026); The Hacker News (Nov 2026); IT Pro (Nov 2026)

## Uplifting Story | sting=feel
// Story 11: Rakia the puppy
[ava] And finally, story eleven. Rakia was just a few months old when a soldier named Camron Shepard found her under an abandoned truck while on patrol in southeastern Europe. He fed her, and the two bonded.
