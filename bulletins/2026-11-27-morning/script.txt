## Cybersecurity | sting=cyber
// Story 8: Cacti zero-day; Q-Dir and DesktopOK supply chain attack
[ava] Story eight, the cyber desk. There's an unpatched flaw in Cacti, the popular open source network monitoring tool. A researcher published a working exploit that goes from no login at all to running commands on the server in just four web requests. It affects the latest version when the guest account is turned on, which isn't the default. There's no CVE or patch yet. Because Cacti often stores network credentials, the advice is to disable guest access and keep it off the open internet.
[ava] And if you use the free Windows utilities Q-Dir or DesktopOK, the South Korean security firm AhnLab says official downloads and updates from the developer's website were poisoned. They quietly installed remote access malware, and uninstalling won't remove it, so run a full security scan if you updated recently.
> source: The Hacker News, "Unpatched Cacti Zero-Day Enables RCE in Just Four HTTP Requests via Guest Access" (Nov 27); watchTowr advisory (Nov 2026); The Hacker News, "Hackers Trojanize Official Q-Dir and DesktopOK Downloads" (Nov 27); AhnLab ASEC (Nov 2026)

## Uplifting Story | sting=feel
// Story 11: Bea Gaddy dinner saved
[ava] And finally, story eleven. In nineteen eighty-one, Bea Gaddy used her lottery winnings to cook Thanksgiving dinner for forty-nine of her Baltimore neighbors. It grew into a city tradition. But this year, a week before Thanksgiving, donations had dried up. Her daughter Cynthia Brooks put it bluntly: there was no dinner, period.
[ava] Then the story aired on WBAL TV, and Chef José Andrés called. His World Central Kitchen brought a food truck, and volunteers from Morgan State's women's basketball team to the mayor pitched in. Hundreds came out in Thursday's storm for the forty-fifth Bea Gaddy dinner, and volunteers delivered meals to people who couldn't make it. As one guest put it, when you don't have a big family, the people here become your family.
> source: WBAL-TV, "Baltimore tradition saved: 45th annual Bea Gaddy Thanksgiving dinner unites community" (Nov 26)
