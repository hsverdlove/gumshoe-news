Now, the top stories here at home.
Inflation picked up in September, with consumer prices rising at a three point seven percent annual rate, driven largely by gasoline, according to the government report covered by the Wall Street Journal and Reuters. Stocks fell on worries the Federal Reserve may raise rates again.
Kimberly Guilfoyle has resigned as U.S. ambassador to Greece, days after the Journal reported she asked a Republican donor to pay off a hundred-thousand-dollar credit card bill.
On the campaign trail, President Trump flew to Alaska to help save a Republican Senate seat, according to the Washington Post, and the Associated Press reports Republicans are pulling back from several front-line House districts. In Maine, Senator Susan Collins debated her Democratic challenger, Troy Jackson.
And in Pennsylvania, a sixth person has died of measles, as cases there top eleven hundred. The C.D.C. is sending more help.
