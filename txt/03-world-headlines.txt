Around the world.
In Serbia, hundreds rallied outside the independent news site Nova dot R S in Belgrade, after new owners told journalists to accept editors from pro-government outlets or leave. Nearly all the staff chose to stay. Parliamentary elections are October twenty-fifth.
In Pakistan, the media regulator has ordered TV channels to stop live coverage of explosions and terror attacks, and to air only government-verified information. It follows emergency rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province.
In India, young protesters have taken to the streets of New Delhi over a nationwide review of voter rolls that critics say removed millions of names. The Election Commission calls it a routine cleanup.
And the Wall Street Journal reports Ukraine says new limits on Starlink are disrupting its military operations.
