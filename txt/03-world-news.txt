Around the world.
The Vatican says Pope Leo the Fourteenth, who is seventy-one, has been diagnosed with lung cancer, and had successful surgery on Tuesday at Rome's Gemelli hospital to remove part of his right lung. His appointments are cancelled through October twentieth.
In the Middle East, Yemen's Houthis attacked the headquarters of Saudi Aramco in Dhahran, causing a fire, after Saudi strikes in Yemen. Reuters reports two ships sank near the Strait of Hormuz after being hit, and Brent crude settled above a hundred and six dollars a barrel.
In Ukraine, Russian strikes left about six hundred thousand people without power, according to President Zelensky. Separately, Ukraine says new Starlink limits are disrupting its drone missions.
And in Congo, the Ebola outbreak has passed nine thousand confirmed cases, the largest in the country's history, while Kenya has reported a second case.
