Time for the market wrap. A rough day on Wall Street after that hot inflation report stirred worries about more interest rate hikes from the Federal Reserve.
According to preliminary figures reported by Reuters, the S and P five hundred fell about eighty-four points, or one point one percent, to seven thousand seven hundred twenty-one. The Nasdaq dropped five hundred three points, or nearly one point nine percent, to twenty-six thousand seven hundred one, with A I names like Oracle and CoreWeave hit hardest. The Dow held up best, slipping just forty-five points, less than a tenth of a percent, to fifty-one thousand six hundred twenty-five. Bank stocks also weighed after quarterly results.
