And now, something to smile about, from the Good News Network.
Twenty-one-year-old Brooklyn Green was heading to the movies when she noticed Mary, a woman who has worked in movie theaters for more than forty-five years, pushing a trash cart. Brooklyn posted a short video and started a fundraiser, hoping for maybe a month's rent. Strangers gave more than a hundred thousand dollars on the first day, and by the next morning the total reached a hundred forty-six thousand. Now Mary gets to find out what rest feels like.
