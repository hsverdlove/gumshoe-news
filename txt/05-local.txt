Closer to home, in Londonderry.
The Londonderry Historical Society hosts an open house this Sunday, October eighteenth, from one to three, with tours of the Morrison House, the Parmenter barn, and the Clark blacksmith shop, plus period military drills with musket firing.
The Londonderry Times reports a busy stretch for local firefighters earlier this month, with two house fires and a medical helicopter call at the high school, and the department is reminding everyone to check their smoke alarms.
And a civic reminder: the general election is Tuesday, November third. New Hampshire closes registration six to thirteen days before, so check with the Town Clerk, or register at your polling place on Election Day.
